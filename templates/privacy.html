Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 16, 2026

Your privacy matters to us. This Privacy Policy explains what information Spendly collects, how we use it, and the choices you have about your data.

1. Information We Collect

We collect only what we need to run the service:

  • Account information — your name, email address and a securely hashed password.
  • Expense data — the amounts, categories, dates and descriptions you enter.
  • Basic technical data — such as browser type and access times, used to keep the service secure and working.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

  • Provide, maintain and improve Spendly.
  • Show you summaries and breakdowns of your spending.
  • Authenticate you and protect your account.
  • Contact you about important changes to the service.

3. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent or trade your personal information. We may share data only when required by law, or with service providers who help us operate Spendly and are bound to keep it confidential.

4. Data Security

We take reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect your data, including password hashing and restricted access. However, no method of storage or transmission is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

5. Cookies and Local Storage

Spendly uses essential cookies to keep you signed in, and your browser's local storage to remember preferences such as your light or dark theme. We do not use advertising or tracking cookies.

6. Your Rights

You can access, update or export your expense data at any time, and you may request deletion of your account and all associated data. Once deleted, your data cannot be recovered.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will revise the "Last updated" date above. Continued use of Spendly after changes take effect means you accept the revised policy.
